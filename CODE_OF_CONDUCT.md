# Code of Conduct

Helix DS adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for everyone taking part in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions
and any other space where the project is represented.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, give and take feedback on the work rather than the person, and
assume good intent. Harassment, insults, and sharing other people's private information are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **martacondedesign@gmail.com**. Every report is reviewed and kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, or ban participants, in line
with the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
