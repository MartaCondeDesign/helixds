# Avatar sample photos

Free stock portraits from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com) — [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/):
free for personal and commercial use, no attribution required. Downloaded at 256×256 (`w=256&h=256&fit=crop`).
Served by Storybook (`staticDirs`) and used for Avatar/AvatarGroup stories. In Figma only the Avatar
component's own `src` layer carries one of them — the files are not laid out on the canvas.

Picked for a flat, single-color background (so they read cleanly at 24–40px inside the circle).
